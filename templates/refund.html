1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your dashboard. Here's what you need to know:

  • Instant Cancellation - Cancel directly from Settings → Billing section
  • No Questions Asked - We respect your decision to cancel
  • Service Access - You retain access until the end of your billing period
  • Data Retention - Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation

2. Refund Eligibility

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. Please review the eligibility criteria:

Scenario Refund Eligibility Time Frame
First-time subscription ✅ Eligible Within 7 days
Renewal payment ❌ Not Eligible N/A
Upgrade (partial month) ❌ Not Eligible Prorated credit
Technical issues ✅ Case-by-case Within 30 days

7-Day Window

Refund requests must be made within 7 days of the original payment date.

Usage-Based

No refunds after significant service usage (e.g., 50+ conversations processed).

Invoice Required

Valid invoice number required for all refund requests.

4. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Used Subscription Periods - Partially used months are non-refundable
  • Add-on Services - One-time setup fees and custom integrations
  • Third-Party Fees - Payment gateway charges (2-3% transaction fee)
  • Enterprise Plans - Custom contracts have separate terms

Important Note

If you've used more than 50% of your monthly chat limit, refunds are automatically declined.

5. Exceptions & Special Cases

We may consider exceptions in the following circumstances:

  • Technical Issues - Prolonged downtime or service unavailability
  • Duplicate Charges - Accidental multiple payments
  • Unauthorized Use - Fraudulent transactions (with proof)
  • Medical/Financial Hardship - Case-by-case review

All exception requests require supporting documentation and are reviewed within 48 hours.

6. How to Request a Refund

Follow these steps to request a refund:

  • Step 1 - Log in to your dashboard and go to Settings → Billing
  • Step 2 - Click on "Request Refund" next to the relevant invoice
  • Step 3 - Fill out the refund request form with reason
  • Step 4 - Submit and wait for confirmation email

Alternatively, email us at refunds@bizflow.ai with your invoice number and reason.
