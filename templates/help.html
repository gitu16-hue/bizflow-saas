Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my WhatsApp number?
Go to Settings → WhatsApp Integration and follow the simple verification process. You'll need a Twilio account for WhatsApp Business API.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, NetBanking, and popular wallets through our secure payment partner Razorpay.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime from your billing settings. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
How does the booking system work?
Customers can book appointments via WhatsApp using natural language. You'll receive instant notifications and can manage all bookings from your dashboard.
