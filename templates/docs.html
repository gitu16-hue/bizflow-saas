Getting Started

Welcome to BizFlow AI! Follow this guide to set up your account and start automating your WhatsApp communications.

1. Create Your Account

Sign up for a free trial account. No credit card required.

2. Complete Onboarding

Fill in your business details, including business type, goals, and working hours.

Tip: Set your business hours accurately to manage customer expectations.

3. Configure WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp Business number through Twilio integration.

# Example Twilio webhook URL
https://bizflow-saas.onrender.com/webhook/whatsapp

API Reference

Integrate BizFlow AI with your existing systems.

Authentication

All API requests require an API key. Get your key from Settings → API.

Endpoints

GET /api/bookings - List all bookings
POST /api/bookings - Create booking
PUT /api/bookings/{id} - Update booking
DELETE /api/bookings/{id} - Delete booking
